ACCA F2 Management Accounting — Standard Formula Sheet

EOQ = √[(2 × Co × D) / Ch]

EBQ = √[(2 × Co × D) / (Ch × (1 - D/R))]

Reorder Level = Maximum usage × Maximum lead time

Minimum Level = Reorder level - (Average usage × Average lead time)

Maximum Level = Reorder level + Reorder qty - (Minimum usage × Minimum lead time)

Rowan Bonus = (Time Saved / Time Allowed) × Time Taken × Basic Rate

Halsey Bonus (50%) = 50% × Time Saved × Basic Rate

Labor Turnover = (Replacements / Average Workforce) × 100%

OAR = Budgeted Overhead / Budgeted Activity

High-Low Variable Cost = (Cost High - Cost Low) / (Units High - Units Low)

Profit Reconciliation: Absorption Profit - Marginal Profit = (Closing Inv - Opening Inv) × Fixed OAR/unit
